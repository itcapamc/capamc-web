Chinese American Parent Association of Montgomery County serves and advocates for
Chinese American families in Montgomery County. Our objective is to promote Chinese
American parent involvement in school communities, to advocate on behalf of our
students and parents, and to establish a strong partnership with all stakeholders to
address educational issues and challenges.
