## Our mission

Our objective is to promote Chinese American parent involvement in school
communities, to advocate on behalf of our students and parents — who are often
first-generation immigrants — and to establish a strong partnership with all
stakeholders to address educational issues and challenges.

Chinese American parental perspectives are frequently absent from educational
discussions. By collaborating with local schools, PTAs, the Board of Education, and
other parent communities, CAPA-MC works to build improved opportunities for all
children while navigating language, cultural, communication, and institutional
barriers.
