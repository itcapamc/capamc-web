CAPA-MC is an authorized certifying organization for the **President's Volunteer
Service Award (PVSA)**, an initiative of the Corporation for National and Community
Service.

Eligible volunteers must be U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents and complete
qualifying service within the April 1 – March 31 period, with at least 20% (minimum
20 hours) coming from CAPA-MC-affiliated club activities. Awards are given at the
Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Lifetime Achievement levels.

Submit completed application forms and documentation to
[pvsa@capamc.org](mailto:pvsa@capamc.org). There is no application fee.
