Applications for the **2026 Dr. Alan Cheung Community Leadership Scholarship** open
on **February 22, 2026**. This year CAPA-MC plans to distribute 15 individual
scholarship awards and 5 group awards, recognizing youth contributions to community
service.

Applicants submit through Google Forms — separate forms for individual and group
applications — and instructions are available in both English and Chinese. The
deadline is **March 22, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET**.

Questions? Email [scholarship@capamc.org](mailto:scholarship@capamc.org).
